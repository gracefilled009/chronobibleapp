Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ChronoBible ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

ChronoBible is designed as an offline-first Bible application. You do not need to register an account or provide any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, or phone number) to use the App.

Data created and stored through your use of the App includes:

  • Reading plan progress and completed daily readings
  • User notes, highlights, and annotations
  • Bookmarked verses and memorization cards
  • Personal prayer journal entries
  • Preferences such as font choice, font scale, theme, and selected Bible translation

2. Data Storage & iCloud Backup

All data is stored directly on your physical device. If you choose to enable the optional iCloud Backup feature, your data is serialized and stored directly in your personal Apple iCloud Drive account. We do not maintain, host, or transfer your data to any external proprietary servers.

3. Tracking and Third Parties

ChronoBible contains no advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, analytics trackers, or third-party data brokers. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

4. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data:

  • You can reset all reading plan progress at any time via App Settings.
  • You can export or delete personal notes and prayer journal entries at any time.
  • Deleting or uninstalling the App from your device will delete all local app data.

5. Children's Privacy

ChronoBible does not collect any personal information from children or adults alike.
